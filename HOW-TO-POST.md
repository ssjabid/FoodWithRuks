# How to post a bake

Ten steps, from your phone or a laptop. Nothing here can break the site: drafts are private until you press Publish, and everything can be edited afterwards.

## 1. Sign in

Go to `/admin/login` and sign in with Google. Only the two allowed accounts get in. If it says the account is not allowed, the address is not on the list yet (Abid can add it).

## 2. The dashboard

The first thing you see is the **Currently** card: four short lines for the front page (Baking, Craving, Last walk, Latest parcel). Change any of them and press Save. Empty lines stay hidden. Under it: how many recipes, stories, comments waiting, messages and email sign-ups there are.

## 3. Start a new bake

Recipes, then New. The form has plain sections; you can fill them in any order and save at any point.

- **The bake.** The name (this becomes the headline, so write it the way you would say it: "Brown butter cookies"), a line for the card, and the story if you want one. The web address is made from the name; leave it alone unless you want to change it.
- **Photos.** Upload straight from the camera roll. The phone shrinks the photo before sending, so it takes a few seconds, not a minute. Write one plain sentence describing the photo for people who cannot see it. You can add a photo to any step too.
- **Where it lives.** Tick a category (Cookies and biscuits, Cakes, Cheesecakes and so on). Meal, dietary notes and occasion are optional. Search words help people find it.
- **Times, how much it makes, the oven.** Prep and bake in minutes. Extra time is chilling, proving, setting or resting. Choose Serves or Makes, then the number, and for Makes say what one of them is called (cookie / cookies). Put the oven in as you use it and tick "fan" if that is your setting; the page works out the rest. Leave the oven empty for a no-bake recipe.
- **Ingredients.** One per row: amount, unit, name. Fractions are fine (1/2). "Group" splits the list into headed parts, such as For the icing.
- **Method.** One step per box. Add a step with the button; steps renumber themselves.
- **The personal bits (optional).** Your note (a few words, shown in handwriting by the name), charms (First try, Travels well, Little helpers, Trail-tested, Make ahead, Taste-tester's pick), the two verdicts, "Inspired by", and how long it keeps. Skip anything you like.

## 4. Save, look, publish

- **Save as draft** keeps it private.
- **Preview** opens the saved draft in a new tab exactly as visitors would see it. Only people with that link can open it.
- **Publish** (or Save and publish) puts it live within a minute. It appears on the front page, in Bakes and in any collection its charms put it in.

To take a bake down, untick Published and save. To fix a typo, edit and save; the site updates itself.

## 5. Photos that look good

Daylight from a window, main lights off, flash off. Wipe the lens. Tap the bake to focus and drag the brightness down a touch. Use the same two or three surfaces (a board, a plate, a tea towel) so the site looks like it has a style. Tall bakes from the side, flat ones from above. Crumbs and a bite taken out are good. One upright photo per bake is the minimum; a wide one and a couple of steps are a bonus.

## 6. Stories

Lifestyle, then New. A title, a cover photo, a category (Days out, Eating out, Travel, Parenting, Craft and hobbies) and the story itself in the editor, which has headings, lists, links and photos. Same Save, Preview and Publish buttons.

## 7. Settings

- **The bake on the front page.** Automatic means your newest; or pin one.
- **Your line under it.** A few handwritten words next to the front-page bake, for example "made these for you x".
- **Lead with a collection.** Puts one collection first on the front page (Ramadan, Eid); it only shows once it has a bake in it.
- **The someday bakery page.** Off by default. On, it shows a hidden page with a waiting list.

## 8. Pages

The About page (title, a line under it, your photo and the text), the line under the front-page bake, and the footer line. The Currently card is here too.

## 9. Comments, messages, sign-ups

Comments wait for you: approve or delete them, and the star rating updates when you approve. Messages from the contact form arrive under Messages. Email sign-ups are under Subscribers, with a download button for a spreadsheet.

## 10. If something looks wrong

Save is never lost: if a form refuses to save, it tells you which field to fix at the top. If the site looks out of date, wait a minute; it refreshes itself. Anything else, tell Abid what you pressed and what you expected.
